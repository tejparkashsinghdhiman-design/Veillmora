Privacy

What this site collects

Veillmora does not ask for or store any personal information. There are no forms, accounts or sign-ups, and the site's own code sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising.

Text on this site uses fonts already on your device, so no font provider is contacted when you visit.

The site is hosted on a third-party platform. Like any host, it may keep standard technical logs such as IP address and browser type. Those logs fall under the host's own policy.

When ordering opens, checkout will be run by Shopify, which will collect the details needed to process an order. This section will be updated with full details before that happens.
